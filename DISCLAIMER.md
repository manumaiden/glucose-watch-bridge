# Disclaimer

## Not a medical device

glucose-watch-bridge is a hobby project shared for educational purposes. It is **not** a medical device, it has **not** been cleared or approved by any regulatory authority, and it must **not** be used to make treatment or dosing decisions.

Readings can be **delayed, missing or wrong**: the pipeline depends on the phone, the network, an unofficial cloud API and your own servers, and any of them can fail silently or with a delay. Always confirm values with the official sensor app or a blood glucose meter, and follow the advice of your healthcare professional.
